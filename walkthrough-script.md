# Five-minute client walkthrough

## 0:00–0:30 — Welcome

“Hi, Dr. Reynolds. Here’s a first draft of your homepage. I’ve carried the structure of the reference site into a calmer, more personal design for your practice. I’ll first walk through the desktop page, then show how the same information reads on a phone.”

## 0:30–1:05 — Hero and opening

“The opening names anxiety and trauma therapy for adults in Santa Monica. A visitor can quickly see the kinds of support you offer and where you practice. The office photograph, warm sand background, and deep blue heading create a quiet first impression, and the consultation button gives a clear next step.”

“The introduction speaks to adults who may appear to be managing while privately feeling anxious or overwhelmed. It reflects the profile’s focus on high-achieving adults without making the person feel labeled.”

## 1:05–1:40 — Who the practice helps

“These three cards show the main reasons someone might reach out: anxiety and panic, the effects of past experiences, and burnout or perfectionism. The short descriptions use everyday language and help visitors recognize their own concerns. The photos use soft landscapes and natural light to keep the page calm.”

## 1:40–2:15 — Focus and approach

“This band brings the key areas of focus together in a quick scan. Below it, the approach section explains how therapy works: it is collaborative, personalized, and paced with care. It names CBT, EMDR, mindfulness, and body-oriented techniques from your profile.”

## 2:15–2:45 — Therapist introduction

“Here visitors meet you and see your Santa Monica location, adult focus, and approach in one place. The portrait makes the page feel personal. The copy emphasizes insight and resilience as part of the work, alongside symptom relief.”

## 2:45–3:20 — Services

“The three service cards expand on anxiety therapy, trauma and EMDR, and burnout and perfectionism counseling. Each description includes relevant local wording naturally and explains what the work may focus on. The layout follows the reference site's specialties section, with a clear hierarchy and consistent spacing.”

## 3:20–3:55 — Our Office and FAQs

“The Our Office section is the new section requested in the assignment. It describes the quiet, private setting with natural light and comfortable seating, and it clarifies the in-person and telehealth options. The two office photos help someone picture the space before a first visit.”

“The FAQs answer initial questions about who you work with, areas of focus, methods, and session formats. They are expandable, so visitors can open just the answer they need.”

## 3:55–4:40 — Mobile walkthrough

“On a phone, the content stacks vertically and the cards become easier to read one at a time. The key headline and consultation button stay near the top. The office gallery and service cards adapt to the narrower screen, and the FAQ answers remain tap-to-open.”

## 4:40–5:00 — Close

“To recap, this draft keeps the original homepage's flow while reshaping it around your adult practice, specialties, methods, and Santa Monica office. Before launch, we would confirm the final booking destination and image permissions. Thank you for taking a look; I’d be glad to hear what feels right and what you’d like refined.”
