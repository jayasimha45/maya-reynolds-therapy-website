About your therapist

Dr. Maya Reynolds, PsyD

Dr. Reynolds is a licensed clinical psychologist based in Santa Monica, California. She works with adults navigating anxiety, chronic stress, and the lingering effects of past experiences—even when they appear high-functioning on the outside.

Her warm, collaborative approach brings together CBT, EMDR, mindfulness-based practices, and body-oriented techniques. She aims to help clients feel more regulated, connected, and resilient.
